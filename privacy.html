Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

Rocco is an iPhone app that turns real house chores into a cozy game. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who helps us run it, how long we keep it and how you can delete it. Rocco is made by Kerem Kaya, an individual developer in Istanbul, Türkiye ("we"). Questions: keremxkaya08@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Your chore photos are checked and then thrown away. We never store the photos.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics tools, and we never sell or share your data for advertising.
  • You can delete your account and its data in the app at any time.

We do not collect your location, contacts, browsing history, advertising identifier or crash diagnostics. Reminders are scheduled on your phone by iOS; we do not send them from a server.

How chore photos work

  • You take the photo inside the app; Rocco never reads your photo library for chores.
  • The first time, the app asks for your permission before any photo is sent to Google's Gemini AI.
  • The photo goes to our server and to the Google Gemini API (paid tier) to check the chore. Google does not use paid-tier requests to train its models. Neither we nor our server keep the photo after the check.
  • We keep a short check record for 60 days: the result, a reason code, a short text note of at most 120 characters (for example "bed made, pillows in place") and fingerprints of the image (mathematical hashes, not the image) so the same photo cannot be used twice. After 60 days the record is deleted.

What other people can see

  • Friends you accept can see your display name, your character and your home. They cannot see your chores, photos, coins or conversations with Rocco.
  • People in your shared home (Home plan) see the shared home, its rooms and items, and each member's name and character.
  • Nobody else can find you. Friends are added only with your personal code, and every request needs your approval.
  • You can remove, block or report anyone. Reports go to us and we review them within 24 hours.

How long we keep data

  • Your account, profile, game data, friends and Ask Rocco history: until you delete your account.
  • Chore check records: 60 days.
  • Reports: until they are resolved, then only as long as needed to keep the community safe.
  • Signing out removes the Ask Rocco conversation from your phone.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Settings > Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and all data above from our server, removes you from your friends' streets and your shared home, disconnects Sign in with Apple, and deletes your record at RevenueCat. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel it in iPhone Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions. You can also email us to delete your data.

Children

Rocco is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher minimum age where you live) and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what data we hold about you, to correct it, to get a copy, or to delete it. Residents of California and other US states with privacy laws have these rights by law. Email keremxkaya08@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days and will not treat you differently for asking.

Users outside the United States

Rocco is available worldwide and its servers are in the United States, so your data is processed there. Our service providers protect these transfers with the safeguards the law requires, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

If you are in the EU, the UK, Türkiye or another country with a similar law, Kerem Kaya is the controller of your data and we rely on these legal bases:

  • Contract: your account, game data, friends, shared home and subscription, which you need to use Rocco.
  • Consent: sending chore photos to Google Gemini. You can turn photo checks off in Settings at any time.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping the community safe (blocks and reports), preventing abuse and controlling AI costs.

You also have the right to object, to restrict processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Each account can read and change only its own data; friends and home members can read only what is listed above.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
