About Rocco

Last updated November 15, 2026

Rocco is an iPhone app that turns real house chores into a daily habit: you do a small real chore, take a photo, Rocco checks it, and the coins you earn furnish a cozy virtual home. Rocco is made by Kerem Kaya, an independent app developer in Istanbul, Türkiye, and has been on the App Store since October 30, 2026.

What is the idea behind Rocco?

Small chores are easy to put off. Rocco makes each chore tiny, about 2 to 5 minutes, and gives it a reward you can see: the coins from a real made bed or a real clean sink turn into a lamp, a rug or a new room in a home that is yours.

  • Real chores earn the home. Every chore is confirmed with a photo, so each piece in your Rocco home stands for something you really did.
  • Coins are never sold. Rocco has no coin packs and no ads. If coins could be bought, the home would stop meaning anything, so coins only come from real chores and are only spent on the home.
  • No guilt. Rocco the raccoon cheers you on and never judges the mess. Miss 3 days and the room just gets a little dusty; nothing is ever lost, and one quest sweeps the dust away. Rocco sends at most one gentle reminder a day.
