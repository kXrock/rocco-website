How much does Rocco cost?

Last updated November 15, 2026. Prices as of November 15, 2026, US App Store.

Rocco is free to download, and using it needs a subscription. Rocco Premium for one person costs $6.99 a month or $29.99 a year, and the Home plan for up to 6 people in one shared home costs $11.99 a month or $49.99 a year. New subscribers get a 7-day free trial on every plan, and cancelling before the trial ends costs nothing.

What does every Rocco plan include?

  • Your own small daily list of 2 to 5 minute chores, with photo checks by Rocco.
  • Coins for every checked chore, to furnish and grow your home from one bedroom to a living room, a kitchen and a bathroom.
  • Chores that are not on your list (20, 35 or 50 coins, up to 3 a day).
  • The weekly promise with gift boxes, and 10 badges with 26 tiers.
  • Ask Rocco, a chat for cleaning and home care questions (up to 15 questions a day).
  • Friends: visit homes on your street and leave stickers or small gifts.
  • Widgets, a timer for tougher chores, and at most one gentle reminder a day.
  • 200 buttons every calendar month for character clothes.
  • No ads, on every plan.

The How it works page explains each of these.

What does the Rocco Home plan add?

The Rocco Home plan lets up to 6 people share one home. Everyone does their own chores, earns their own coins and moves their own furniture. One person subscribes, and the people they invite join with a home code and do not pay.

What can I try in Rocco before subscribing?

Before the subscription screen, a new Rocco user can do the first quest for free: make your bed, take the photo, and Rocco checks it for 20 coins. You then pick one welcome item for your room at 90% off. After that, the 7-day free trial lets you try everything.

Can I buy coins in Rocco?

No. Coins in Rocco are never sold: there are no coin packs and no ads. Coins are only earned with real chores and only spent on your home.

What are buttons in Rocco?

Buttons are an optional second currency in Rocco that is only for character clothes. Every plan includes 200 buttons each calendar month, 18 of the 46 clothing pieces are free, and buttons never turn into coins. If you want more, there are three packs:

How do the Rocco free trial and billing work?

Every Rocco plan starts with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. Apple handles the payment through your Apple Account. With notifications on, Rocco reminds you 2 days before the trial ends. If you do not cancel before the trial ends, the plan you chose starts and renews until you cancel.

How do I cancel Rocco?

To cancel Rocco, open iPhone Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions > Rocco and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel before the trial ends and you pay nothing. After cancelling, you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Can I get a refund for Rocco?

Apple handles all Rocco payments, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How much does Rocco cost outside the US?

Outside the US, Rocco costs Apple's local price for each country, which is lower in many countries. Rocco is available in 175 countries and regions, and the App Store shows the exact price for your country before you subscribe.

Is there a free version of Rocco?

No, Rocco has no free plan. Rocco is free to download, the first quest can be done before subscribing, and every plan comes with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. More answers are in the Rocco FAQ, and the About page explains why coins are never sold.
