House cleaning checklist: a room-by-room list

By Kerem Kaya, maker of Rocco

Last updated November 21, 2026

A house cleaning checklist lists every task in the order you do it, room by room and top to bottom: dust first, wipe surfaces, then do the floors last. For a small home it takes about 1.5 to 2.5 hours in total, or about 20 to 40 minutes a room. This page has a tick-off list for the kitchen, bathroom, bedroom and living room, plus the high-touch spots and monthly jobs.

Key takeaways

  • Work room by room, top to bottom: dust high, then wipe surfaces, then floors last.
  • A standard clean of a small home takes about 1.5 to 2.5 hours. Split it over a week if that is too long.
  • Clean first. For most homes the CDC says regular cleaning is enough, and disinfecting is mainly for when someone is sick.
  • Tick items off as you go. Print the page or keep it open on your phone.

What is on a house cleaning checklist?

This checklist covers a full, standard clean of a small home. Use it once a week, or split the rooms across the week. For the day-by-day version, see the weekly cleaning schedule.

What should I do before I start?

  • Open a window, put on music and set a timer for the first room.
  • Pick up clutter and put things back where they live. Cleaning is faster on clear surfaces.
  • Gather everything in one caddy: all-purpose cleaner, glass cleaner, cloths, a scrub brush, trash bags.
  • Start the laundry so it runs while you clean.

What is the kitchen cleaning checklist?

About 30 to 40 minutes.

  • Clear the counters and wipe them down.
  • Wipe the stovetop, microwave and the fronts of appliances.
  • Clean the sink and drain area.
  • Wipe cabinet fronts and handles.
  • Check the fridge: throw out old food and wipe spills.
  • Empty the trash and recycling.
  • Sweep and mop the floor last.

The US Food and Drug Administration advises wiping up fridge spills immediately and washing cutting boards and counters with hot, soapy water after preparing each food item. The UConn Extension suggests going through the fridge at least weekly.

What is the bathroom cleaning checklist?

About 25 to 30 minutes.

  • Spray the toilet, shower or tub and sink with cleaner and let it sit while you do the next steps.
  • Wipe the mirror and the counter.
  • Scrub the sink, then the shower or tub, then the toilet last.
  • Wipe the toilet seat, handle and the base.
  • Swap the hand towel and bath mat if they need it.
  • Empty the trash.
  • Mop the floor.

What is the bedroom cleaning checklist?

About 15 to 20 minutes per bedroom.

  • Strip the bed and put on fresh sheets.
  • Put away clothes, and take laundry to the hamper.
  • Dust the nightstands, dresser and headboard.
  • Clear the nightstand of cups and clutter.
  • Vacuum, including along the edges.

What is the living room cleaning checklist?

About 20 to 25 minutes.

  • Put away anything that does not belong: cups, mail, blankets, remotes.
  • Dust shelves, the TV stand and picture frames.
  • Wipe coffee tables and side tables.
  • Fluff cushions and shake out throws.
  • Vacuum the floor and under the sofa edges.

What high-touch spots should I wipe?

The CDC gives light switches, doorknobs and countertops as examples of high-touch surfaces and says to clean them regularly and after visitors come over.

  • Light switches
  • Doorknobs and cabinet handles
  • Fridge and oven handles
  • Remote controls and phone cases
  • Faucet handles

What is the whole-house finish?

  • Take out all the trash and recycling.
  • Vacuum all the rooms, working toward the door.
  • Mop the hard floors, starting at the far end of the house.
  • Put the cleaning caddy away and the used cloths in the wash.

What about the monthly and deep-clean items?

Every few weeks, add a few of these to your normal clean. N.C. Cooperative Extension lists them as monthly: wash blankets and comforters, vacuum curtains, clean appliances, wipe cabinets, clean out the pantry, and dust light fixtures and blinds. Every three months or so: windows and screens, shower curtains and pillows. Once a year: a deeper clean, plus rugs and upholstery.

  • Wash blankets and comforters
  • Clean inside the microwave and the oven door
  • Deep clean the fridge
  • Wipe cabinets and clean out the pantry
  • Dust light fixtures, blinds and vents
  • Wash windows and screens (every 3 months)

How do I make the checklist faster?

  1. Do one room at a time. Finish a room before you start the next, so the home looks better as you go.
  2. Work top to bottom. Dust first, then surfaces, then floors, so dirt falls where you have not cleaned yet.
  3. Let cleaner sit. Spray the bathroom first and wipe it after you have done something else.
  4. Split it up. One room a day takes about 20 to 40 minutes. N.C. Cooperative Extension suggests spacing the work over time instead of cramming it into one day.

Questions people ask

How long does a full house cleaning take?

For a small home with one bathroom, a standard clean using this checklist takes about 1.5 to 2.5 hours for one person. Splitting it over several days keeps each session between 20 and 40 minutes.

In what order should I clean a house?

Clean room by room, and inside each room work from top to bottom: dust high shelves first, then wipe surfaces, then do the floors last. Start with the rooms that need the longest soak time, like the bathroom.

Do I need to disinfect everything?

For most homes, no. The CDC says regular cleaning removes most germs from surfaces and that disinfecting is likely not needed at home unless someone is sick. Clean high-touch surfaces like light switches and doorknobs regularly.

What should I clean every day?

Make the bed, wash the dishes after meals, wipe the kitchen counters after cooking and wipe up spills right away. See the daily cleaning routine guide for a 15 to 20 minute version.
