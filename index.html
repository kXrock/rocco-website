Real chores, cozy rewards

Rocco turns real house chores into a cozy game. Do a chore, snap a photo, earn coins and decorate a home that is all yours.

How it works

  • A tiny plan every day, sized to the minutes you have.
  • Snap a photo when you are done. Rocco the raccoon checks it and pays you in coins.
  • Spend coins on cozy decor, grow your home and make your character.
  • Stuck on a stain? Ask Rocco.
  • Visit friends' homes on your street, or share a home with the people you live with.

Rocco is for iPhone, in the United States. Coins are never sold, only earned. A subscription and sign-in are required.
