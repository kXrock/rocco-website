Good to know

Do I need a subscription?

Yes. Rocco requires a subscription. New subscribers start with a 7-day free trial, and you can cancel anytime in your Apple Account settings. Cancel before the trial ends and you pay nothing.

What happens to my chore photos?

You take the photo inside the app. An AI service (Google Gemini) checks it, then it is thrown away, and Rocco never stores your photos. The app asks for your permission before the first photo is checked. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Can the people I live with join in?

Yes. With the Home plan, up to 6 people can live in one shared home. Everyone does their own chores and earns their own coins.

Is Rocco only for iPhone?

Yes, Rocco is for iPhone for now.

Will Rocco judge my mess?

Never. Quests are tiny, steps are short, and you get at most one gentle reminder a day at a time you choose.
