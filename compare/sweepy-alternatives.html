What are good alternatives to Sweepy?

Last updated November 15, 2026

Three apps to look at besides Sweepy are Tody, FlyLady and Rocco. Tody goes by how much each task needs doing instead of fixed dates, FlyLady follows a ready-made routine method, and Rocco turns chores into tiny photo-checked quests that build a virtual home. The right pick depends on what kept you from sticking with a schedule before.

The short answer

  • If a fixed schedule felt like a list of things you were behind on, look at Tody: it goes by how much each task needs doing, with no deadlines or alarms by default.
  • If you wanted a clear routine to follow, look at FlyLady: ready-made morning, afternoon and evening routines and zone-by-zone cleaning lists.
  • If you planned well but kept putting the chores off, look at Rocco: tiny 2 to 5 minute chores, a photo check and coins that furnish a virtual home.
  • If Sweepy's plan suited you and something else got in the way, Sweepy itself may still be worth another try.

What does Sweepy do, for reference?

Sweepy is a house cleaning schedule that tracks each room, puts the most needed tasks first and spreads the work among the people in the home. It is free to download, lists a subscription at $3.99 a month or $19.99 a year with a 3-day free trial, runs on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Android, and is rated 4.7 from more than 10,000 ratings (US App Store, November 15, 2026). Sweepy on the App Store.

Tody

  • What it does: A to-do list for cleaning routines that goes by how much each task actually needs doing, not fixed dates. It shows how dirty each area is getting. No deadlines or alarms by default; notifications are optional.
  • Price: Free to download. In-app purchases listed: Premium Solo $9.99, Premium+ Duo $24.99, Premium+ Family $39.99, Premium+ Team $79.99 (US App Store, November 15, 2026). The billing period is not stated on its App Store page.
  • Platforms: iPhone and iPad (iOS 15.0 or later) and Android, in 10 languages.
  • Track record: first released in January 2013, rated 4.8 from more than 10,000 ratings.
  • Who it suits: people who want to clean by need rather than by date and skip deadlines, alone or in a large household.

Tody on the App Store

FlyLady

  • What it does: Follows the FlyLady Method by Marla Cilley: ready-made morning, afternoon and evening routines, room-by-room cleaning lists organized by zone, weekly reminders and daily inspiration. You can add your own routines and lists. It also has points and achievements.
  • Price: Free to download; its description says you can start free with no login. Subscriptions are listed at $1.99 a week, $3.99 a month or $37.99 a year (US App Store, November 15, 2026).
  • Platforms: iPhone (iOS 15.0 or later) and Android, in English.
  • Track record: first released in May 2016, rated 4.9 from more than 20,000 ratings.
  • Who it suits: people who want a complete method to follow, with routines for each part of the day and daily inspiration.

FlyLady on the App Store

Rocco

  • What it does: builds a short daily list of tiny real chores of about 2 to 5 minutes. You take a photo, Rocco checks it with AI, and you earn coins that furnish and grow a virtual home.
  • Price: free to download, but a subscription is required and there is no free plan: $6.99 a month or $29.99 a year, or the Home plan for up to 6 people at $11.99 a month or $49.99 a year (US App Store, November 15, 2026). Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial, and you can try the first quest before subscribing.
  • Platforms: iPhone only (iOS 16.4 or later), in 10 languages.
  • Track record: released on October 30, 2026, so it has few or no App Store ratings yet.
  • Who it suits: people who keep putting off small chores, have busy weeks or low-energy days, and like seeing a reward grow.

How do I pick?

Think about what made Sweepy stop working for you. If it was the dates, try an app that goes by need. If it was not knowing where to start, try a ready-made routine. If it was starting at all, try making each chore smaller and rewarding it. Each of these apps is free to download, so you can try one before you pay. Questions people ask

Is there a free alternative to Sweepy?

Tody and FlyLady are both free to download, and FlyLady's App Store description says you can start free with no login. Tody's App Store page does not state which features are free. Rocco is free to download but needs a subscription after a 7-day free trial, and it has no free plan.

Is there a Sweepy alternative for Android?

Tody and FlyLady are both on Google Play. Rocco is iPhone only.

Which Sweepy alternatives work for a shared home?

In Tody, household members can sync plans across devices and check in to claim credit. Rocco's Home plan lets up to 6 people share one home in the app, and people you invite do not pay. FlyLady's App Store page does not state household sharing.
