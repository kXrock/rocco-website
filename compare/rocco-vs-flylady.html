Rocco vs FlyLady: what is the difference?

Last updated November 15, 2026

FlyLady follows the FlyLady Method by Marla Cilley, with ready-made daily routines and cleaning lists organized by zone, and you can start free with no login. Rocco makes each chore tiny, checks it with a photo and rewards it with coins that furnish a virtual home, and it needs a subscription after a 7-day free trial. In short, FlyLady is built around a named method and daily routines, and Rocco is built around doing one small chore at a time.

The short answer

  • FlyLady: the FlyLady Method in an app, with daily routines and zones. Free to start, also on Android, since May 2016.
  • Rocco: tiny chores, each confirmed with a photo, with coins that furnish a virtual home. iPhone only, subscription required, released October 30, 2026.
  • FlyLady's listed monthly subscription costs less than Rocco's (US App Store, November 15, 2026).

Choose FlyLady if...

  • You want a complete, ready-made method to follow, the FlyLady Method by Marla Cilley, with daily inspiration.
  • You like routines for the morning, afternoon and evening, and cleaning one zone of the home at a time.
  • You want to start free with no login, or you like a weekly option at $1.99 or a monthly price of $3.99 (US App Store, November 15, 2026).
  • You use an Android phone, or you like a long track record: on the App Store since May 2016, rated 4.9 from more than 20,000 ratings.

Choose Rocco if...

  • You keep putting off chores and want each one to be small, about 2 to 5 minutes.
  • You like proof that a chore is really done: every chore is confirmed with a photo that Rocco checks.
  • A reward you can see keeps you going: coins furnish and grow a cozy virtual home that is yours.
  • You want help with cleaning questions: Ask Rocco answers up to 15 a day, and you can add a photo.

Where each app has the edge

FlyLady is free to start with no login, its listed monthly subscription costs less, it runs on Android, and it has been on the App Store since May 2016 with many ratings. Rocco runs on iPhone only, needs a subscription and has no free plan. It is also new: it was released on October 30, 2026, so it has few or no App Store ratings yet. Where Rocco is different: tiny quests, a photo check for every chore, coins that build a home, and Ask Rocco.

Can I use both?

Yes. FlyLady's routines and zones can give your week a shape, and Rocco can turn one small chore at a time into a photo and a reward. That is a reasonable combination, not a requirement; if you subscribe to both, you pay for both.

Questions people ask

Is FlyLady free?

FlyLady is free to download, and its App Store description says you can start free with no login and that subscribing unlocks every feature. Subscriptions are listed at $1.99 a week, $3.99 a month or $37.99 a year (US App Store, November 15, 2026).

Is Rocco free?

Rocco is free to download, but a subscription is required and there is no free plan: $6.99 a month or $29.99 a year (US App Store, November 15, 2026). Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial, and you can try the first quest before subscribing.

Which languages do they support?

FlyLady's App Store page lists English. Rocco is in 10 languages: English, Turkish, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese.
