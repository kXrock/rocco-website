Rocco vs Sweepy: what is the difference?

Last updated November 15, 2026

Sweepy plans and schedules the cleaning of the whole home, spreads the work among the people you live with, and is free to download. Rocco makes each chore tiny, checks it with a photo and rewards it with coins that furnish a virtual home, and it needs a subscription after a 7-day free trial. In short, Sweepy is built around planning the whole home, and Rocco is built around doing one small chore at a time.

The short answer

  • Sweepy: a cleaning schedule for the whole home and everyone in it. Free to download, on more devices, since March 2020.
  • Rocco: tiny chores, each confirmed with a photo, with coins that furnish a virtual home. iPhone only, subscription required, released October 30, 2026.
  • Sweepy's listed subscription costs less than Rocco's (US App Store, November 15, 2026).

Choose Sweepy if...

  • You want one plan for the whole home, room by room, with the tasks that need cleaning most shown first.
  • You share your home and want the work spread among everyone, with a daily schedule made for each person automatically.
  • You want to start free, or you prefer a lower price: a subscription listed at $3.99 a month or $19.99 a year (US App Store, November 15, 2026).
  • You use an iPad, an Apple Watch or an Android phone, or you like a long track record: on the App Store since March 2020, rated 4.7 from more than 10,000 ratings.

Choose Rocco if...

  • You keep putting off chores and want each one to be small, about 2 to 5 minutes.
  • You like proof that a chore is really done: every chore is confirmed with a photo that Rocco checks.
  • A reward you can see keeps you going: coins furnish and grow a cozy virtual home that is yours.
  • You want help with cleaning questions: Ask Rocco answers up to 15 a day, and you can add a photo.

Where each app has the edge

Sweepy is free to download, its listed subscription costs less, it runs on more devices and it has a long record with many ratings. Rocco runs on iPhone only, needs a subscription and has no free plan. It is also new: it was released on October 30, 2026, so it has few or no App Store ratings yet. Where Rocco is different: tiny quests, a photo check for every chore, coins that build a home, and Ask Rocco.

Can I use both?

Yes. A scheduler like Sweepy can keep track of what the whole home needs, and Rocco can turn one small chore at a time into a photo and a reward. That is a reasonable combination, not a requirement; if you subscribe to both, you pay for both.

Questions people ask

Is Sweepy free?

Sweepy is free to download. Its App Store page lists a subscription at $3.99 a month or $19.99 a year with a 3-day free trial (US App Store, November 15, 2026). The page does not state exactly which features are free.

Is Rocco free?

Rocco is free to download, but a subscription is required and there is no free plan: $6.99 a month or $29.99 a year (US App Store, November 15, 2026). Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial, and you can try the first quest before subscribing.

Which one works for a shared home?

Both do. Sweepy spreads the workload among the people in the home and makes a daily schedule for each member. Rocco's Home plan lets up to 6 people share one home in the app, for $11.99 a month or $49.99 a year (US App Store, November 15, 2026), and people you invite do not pay.
