Which chore and cleaning app fits me: Rocco, Sweepy, Tody or FlyLady?

Last updated November 15, 2026

Pick the app that matches what usually stops you. Sweepy builds a cleaning schedule for the whole home and spreads it among the people you live with. Tody goes by how much each task needs doing instead of fixed dates. FlyLady follows a ready-made method with daily routines. Rocco makes each chore tiny, checks it with a photo and rewards it with coins that furnish a virtual home.

The short answer

  • Sweepy plans the whole home and spreads the work among the people you live with.
  • Tody goes by how much each task needs doing, with no deadlines or alarms by default.
  • FlyLady follows a ready-made method with routines for each part of the day.
  • Rocco makes each chore tiny, checks it with a photo and rewards it with a home you furnish.
  • Sweepy, Tody and FlyLady are free to download and also run on Android. Rocco is iPhone only and needs a subscription after a 7-day free trial.

Which one should I choose?

Choose Sweepy if...

  • You want one plan for the whole home, room by room, with the tasks that need cleaning most shown first.
  • You share your home and want the work spread among everyone, with a daily schedule for each person.
  • You use an iPad, an Apple Watch or an Android phone.
  • You want to start free, or you prefer a subscription listed at $3.99 a month or $19.99 a year (US App Store, November 15, 2026).

Choose Tody if...

  • You want to clean by need instead of a fixed calendar, and see how dirty each area is getting.
  • You prefer no deadlines or alarms by default.
  • You use an iPad or an Android phone.
  • You want an app that can be set up for one person or a large household, with check-ins to claim credit.

Choose FlyLady if...

  • You want a complete, ready-made method to follow: the FlyLady Method by Marla Cilley.
  • You like morning, afternoon and evening routines and cleaning one zone at a time.
  • You use an Android phone.
  • You want to start free with no login, or you like a weekly option at $1.99 (US App Store, November 15, 2026).

Choose Rocco if...

  • You keep putting off chores and want each one to be small, about 2 to 5 minutes.
  • You like proof that a chore is really done: every chore is confirmed with a photo that Rocco checks.
  • A reward you can see keeps you going: coins furnish and grow a cozy virtual home.
  • You want help with cleaning questions: Ask Rocco answers up to 15 a day.

Plainly: Sweepy, Tody and FlyLady are free to download, run on Android, and have been on the App Store for years with many ratings. Rocco runs on iPhone only, needs a subscription and has no free plan. It is also new: it was released on October 30, 2026, so it has few or no App Store ratings yet.

Questions people ask

Which of these apps checks that a chore is really done?

Rocco confirms every chore with a photo checked by AI. In Sweepy and Tody you mark a chore done in the app, and in FlyLady you check it off; none of their App Store pages describes a photo check.

Which of these apps are free?

Sweepy, Tody and FlyLady are free to download, and FlyLady's App Store description says you can start free with no login. Sweepy's and Tody's App Store pages do not state exactly which features are free. Rocco is free to download but needs a subscription after a 7-day free trial, and it has no free plan.

Which ones work on Android?

Sweepy, Tody and FlyLady are on Google Play. Rocco is iPhone only.

Which ones work for a household?

Sweepy spreads the workload among the people in the home with a daily schedule for each member. In Tody, household members can sync plans and check in to claim credit. Rocco's Home plan lets up to 6 people share one home in the app, and people you invite do not pay. FlyLady's App Store page does not state household sharing.
