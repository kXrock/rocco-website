Rocco vs Tody: what is the difference?

Last updated November 15, 2026

Tody is a cleaning to-do list that goes by how much each task actually needs doing rather than fixed dates, and it is free to download. Rocco makes each chore tiny, checks it with a photo and rewards it with coins that furnish a virtual home, and it needs a subscription after a 7-day free trial. In short, Tody is built around knowing what needs doing, and Rocco is built around doing one small chore at a time.

The short answer

  • Tody: cleaning by need, not by calendar. Free to download, on more devices, since January 2013.
  • Rocco: tiny chores, each confirmed with a photo, with coins that furnish a virtual home. iPhone only, subscription required, released October 30, 2026.
  • Tody has no deadlines or alarms by default. Rocco sends at most one gentle note a day.

Choose Tody if...

  • You want to clean by need instead of a fixed calendar, and see at a glance how dirty each area is getting.
  • You prefer no deadlines or alarms by default, with notifications only if you want them.
  • You want to start free: Tody is free to download.
  • You use an iPad or an Android phone, or you like a long track record: on the App Store since January 2013, rated 4.8 from more than 10,000 ratings.

Choose Rocco if...

  • You keep putting off chores and want each one to be small, about 2 to 5 minutes.
  • You like proof that a chore is really done: every chore is confirmed with a photo that Rocco checks.
  • A reward you can see keeps you going: coins furnish and grow a cozy virtual home that is yours.
  • You want help with cleaning questions: Ask Rocco answers up to 15 a day, and you can add a photo.

Where each app has the edge

Tody is free to download, runs on iPad and Android, can be set up for one person or a large household, and has been on the App Store since January 2013 with many ratings. Rocco runs on iPhone only, needs a subscription and has no free plan. It is also new: it was released on October 30, 2026, so it has few or no App Store ratings yet. Where Rocco is different: tiny quests, a photo check for every chore, coins that build a home, and Ask Rocco.

Can I use both?

Yes. Tody can show you which areas need attention, and Rocco can turn one small chore at a time into a photo and a reward. That is a reasonable combination, not a requirement; if you pay for both, you pay twice.

Questions people ask

Is Tody free?

Tody is free to download. Its App Store page lists in-app purchases: Premium Solo $9.99, Premium+ Duo $24.99, Premium+ Family $39.99 and Premium+ Team $79.99 (US App Store, November 15, 2026). The page does not state the billing period or which features are free.

Is Rocco free?

Rocco is free to download, but a subscription is required and there is no free plan: $6.99 a month or $29.99 a year (US App Store, November 15, 2026). Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial, and you can try the first quest before subscribing.

Which one works for a shared home?

Both do. In Tody, household members can sync plans across devices and check in to claim credit. Rocco's Home plan lets up to 6 people share one home in the app, for $11.99 a month or $49.99 a year (US App Store, November 15, 2026), and people you invite do not pay.
